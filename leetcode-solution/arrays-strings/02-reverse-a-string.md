## Problem: Reverse a String (Easy)
**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-string/

### Approach
Used a two-pointer approach (one starting from the beginning and the other from the end) to swap characters in-place until they meet in the middle.

### Complexity
- Time: $O(N)$
- Space: $O(1)$

### Notes
Learned how to efficiently manipulate character arrays in-place using two pointers.
